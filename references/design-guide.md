# Optional design references

These examples describe two successful directions in this series. Use them as inspiration, not fixed layouts or evidence about a new photo.

## Floral restaurant table

- Scene details: green floral plates, ribbed green glasses, chopsticks and rests, ivory bowls, dark-green menu with pink flowers, lidded takeaway cup.
- Palette: warm ivory, forest green, dusty pink.
- Title: “a little table, a lot to say” / “一张餐桌，满满的实用英语”.
- Useful functions: browsing a menu, ordering a drink, asking for a recommendation, sharing a meal.
- Vocabulary examples: table setting / 餐桌布置; floral pattern / 花卉图案; chopstick rest / 筷架; menu cover / 菜单封面.
- Accuracy: a coloured drink does not reveal its exact ingredients. A floral menu cover does not establish the restaurant's cuisine.

## Roast duck meal

- Scene details: sliced roast duck on an oval platter, thin pancakes in a bamboo steamer, a bowl of leafy greens, porcelain spoon and blue-and-white tableware.
- Palette: creamy paper, tea brown, porcelain blue, bamboo tan, restrained leafy green.
- Title: “wrap, share & enjoy” / “一口烤鸭，聊出地道英语”.
- Useful functions: describing food, explaining how to make a wrap, passing dishes, inviting others to help themselves.
- Expressions: wrap duck in a pancake / 用薄饼包鸭肉; roll up a pancake / 把薄饼卷起来; help yourself to some duck / 自己夹些鸭肉吃，别客气.
- Accuracy: avoid asserting a specific regional duck recipe, unseen sauce, or exact vegetable variety. Do not visually substitute dumplings or rice for the pancakes.
